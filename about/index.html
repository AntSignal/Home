About Ant Signal

Technology for
intentional time.

Ant Signal LLC is a technology company building software that helps people set stronger boundaries with their screens.

Our first product is Purpose, an accountability and focus app for iPhone. It combines schedules, layered protections, and optional support from someone you trust—without turning accountability into surveillance.

Our point of view

Good tools make the right choice easier to keep.

Attention is shaped by environments, defaults, and other people—not only by self-control. We build systems that make intentions concrete while keeping the user informed and in control.
